People behind the workshop.

Sijia Liu

Sijia Liu is a PhD candidate at the School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong. Her research examines generative AI and creative practices from HCI and curatorial perspectives, including human-AI co-creation, artistic workflows, and the translation of computational processes into physical and exhibited outcomes. She has experience organizing and facilitating creative workshops, artist residencies, exhibitions, and participatory creative research.

sijialiu7-c@my.cityu.edu.hk

Yanheng Li

Yanheng Li is a PhD candidate at the School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong. Her work explores how machines, from embodied robots to AI agents, can communicate and express themselves through different modalities and forms, including movement, visual and textual interaction, and how people perceive and engage with these machine expressions. Her research draws extensively on co-design and workshop-based methods to engage participants in imagining and exploring emerging forms of human–machine interaction. She also has experience organizing and facilitating academic workshops, including at ACM DIS.

Amy Smith

Amy Smith is a Computer Science PhD candidate at Queen Mary University of London (IGGI programme). She holds an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Birmingham and a BA in Fine Art from Bath School of Art and Design, and spent several years working as a commercial tattoo artist. Her research is focused on the intersection between HCI, generative deep learning and computational creativity, exploring how we can meaningfully discuss user prompting chains - sent to AI over time - to gain a deeper understanding of how users are interacting with GenAI technologies. The work also examines whether media produced by GenAI can be used to provoke and stimulate the imaginative, ideation and visualisation capabilities of the user in creative tasks. Her academic work has been published at CHI, ACM Creativity & Cognition, AAAI, SIGGRAPH Asia, ICCC and Science.

Yifan Zhao

Yifan Zhao is an HCI researcher and creative practitioner working with VR, immersive media, and generative AI. Her practice explores how emerging technologies can support creative expression through immersive and interactive experiences. She has experience developing VR environments and experimental media to explore themes such as death, dying, and mortality, as well as facilitating creative workshops and collaborative making activities.

RAY LC

RAY LC is Assistant Professor of Creative Media at Studio for Narrative Spaces, City University of Hong Kong. His practice explores how humans adapt to constraints to foster creativity, particularly through co-creation with AI and machines. He uses human-computer interaction and speculative narrative approaches to examine how emerging technologies reshape creative expression, human-machine relationships, and social practices.
